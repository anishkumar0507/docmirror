About Us

The Doc Mirror helps doctors see themselves the way patients do — across Google Search, Google Maps, and AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude — and gives them a clear path to being found first.

What is The Doc Mirror

The Doc Mirror is a visibility platform built for medical practices. In about 60 seconds, it analyses how a doctor appears across the places patients actually search today — Google, Maps, online reviews, and AI assistants — and turns that into a single Doctor Visibility Score with specific, prioritised fixes.

We do not touch medical records or patient data. We focus on one thing: making sure that when a patient is looking for care, the right doctor is easy to find, trust, and book.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To give every doctor a clear, honest picture of their online visibility — and the practical tools to improve it — without needing a marketing agency or technical expertise.

Our Vision

A world where patients are matched with the right care because great doctors are visible everywhere patients search, from traditional search engines to AI assistants.

Why We Exist

Patients no longer just Google their doctor. A growing share now ask AI tools to recommend one. Yet most excellent physicians are nearly invisible in these new channels — outranked by competitors simply because their online presence is incomplete or out of date.

Marketing agencies charge hundreds of dollars to diagnose these gaps, and the reports are often too technical to act on. The Doc Mirror was built to close that gap: an instant, affordable, plain-English visibility check that any practice can run and act on the same day.

Helping Doctors Improve Visibility

Beyond the free snapshot, The Doc Mirror offers a full audit and an ongoing monitoring subscription with weekly checks, live alerts, competitor tracking, an AI growth engine, and a live dashboard. The goal is simple: turn online visibility into more of the right patients, every month.
