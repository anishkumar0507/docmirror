Production & Compliance Guide

How to shoot quality content with what you already have, how to write captions that convert, and what every {{SPECIALTY}} must avoid under {{COMPLIANCE_FRAMEWORK}}.

Shoot production essentials

☀
Lighting
Face a window for natural light. Avoid overhead fluorescent. A {{CURRENCY_SYMBOL}}2,000 ring light covers 90% of shoots.
▢
Framing
Shoot vertical (9:16) for Reels and Shorts. Phone at chest height. Keep some negative space above your head.
●
Wardrobe & Background
White coat with stethoscope visible. Avoid pure white background with white coat — use a navy/colour wall. No busy patterns.
♪
Audio
Phone mic works close-up. A {{CURRENCY_SYMBOL}}1,500 lavalier mic transforms quality. Always do an audio test before filming.

Caption framework — hook, value, CTA

HookFirst 1–2 lines. Must grab attention in 1 second. "5 silent signs your heart is sending warnings"
Value3–5 lines of useful information patients can act on. Educational, not promotional.
CTAOne clear ask. "Save this post · Book consultation · Follow for more"
DisclaimerSmall at the end. "Educational content only. Consult a doctor for personal advice."
Hashtags5–10 total. Mix: location (#{{HASHTAG_LOCATION}}) + specialty (#{{HASHTAG_SPECIALTY}}) + condition + brand.

Universal compliance — never publish content that

Claims to cure, guarantee, or promise specific outcomes ("100% success", "money-back guarantee", "no side effects")
Uses comparative or superlative language ("best cardiologist", "better than any other clinic")
Shows identifiable patients (face, name, voice) without explicit written consent on file
Attributes outcomes to specific procedures without statistical context and source citation
Promotes non-medical products (supplements, devices) the practice does not endorse clinically
Advises patients to stop prescribed medications or skip recommended tests
