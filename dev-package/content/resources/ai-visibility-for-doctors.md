Patients no longer start every search on Google. A growing share open ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude and ask, in plain language, *"who is a good dermatologist near me?"* If the AI does not know your name, you are invisible to that patient — no matter how good your website is.

## Why AI visibility is different from SEO

Classic SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links. AI visibility optimizes for whether a model *recommends you by name* inside a written answer. The mechanics are related but not identical.

- **SEO** rewards on-page keywords, backlinks, and crawlability.
- **AI visibility** rewards being a well-established, well-cited *entity* across the sources models trust.

## The three pillars of AI visibility

### 1. Presence

Are you listed, completely and consistently, on the directories AI models are trained on? Incomplete or conflicting listings weaken your entity.

### 2. Authority

Are you cited in content the model considers trustworthy — medical publishers, news outlets, journals, hospital pages?

### 3. Consistency

Do your name, specialty, and location match everywhere? Conflicting facts make a model less confident, and less confident means less likely to recommend.

## A simple self-check

1. Open ChatGPT and Gemini.
2. Ask each for the best doctor in your specialty and city.
3. Note whether you appear, and who does.
4. Repeat for two or three nearby cities.

> If competitors appear and you do not, the gap is almost always in *presence* and *citations* — not in the quality of your care.
